### 3.1 Review Procedure

For each deliverable:

1. Identify the task owner and corresponding Sprint 2 task ID.
2. Open the related GitHub Issue and Pull Request.
3. Confirm that the required documentation or design artifact exists.
4. Check applicable acceptance criteria and reviewer feedback.
5. Verify that required approvals and CI checks have been completed.
6. Record any incomplete, rejected, or deferred items.
7. Assign follow-up actions to the responsible team member.

**Acceptance Rule:** An item must not be recorded as accepted merely because its PR exists or its automated checks pass.

### 4.1 Retrospective Guidelines

- Give all team members an opportunity to contribute.
- Focus on processes and outcomes rather than personal blame.
- Distinguish verified problems from assumptions.
- Record actionable improvements with identified owners.
- Do not publish confidential peer-evaluation details.
- Record the final outcomes only after the retrospective is conducted.
